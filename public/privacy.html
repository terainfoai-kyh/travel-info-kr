Privacy Policy (개인정보처리방침)

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Welcome to VORA AI (accessible at https://koreatravel.cc). VORA AI operates as an intelligent tourism concierge platform providing real-time personalized travel routes, weather outfits, and cultural curation across South Korea using open public data from the Korea Tourism Organization (TourAPI 4.0). We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and digital trust of our global visitors.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (No PII Required)

VORA AI is intentionally designed as a frictionless, free public service. You can generate multi-day itineraries and explore Korean landmarks without creating an account or providing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, passport number, or phone number.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense Advertising Policies

Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendors Disclosure:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Web Analytics and Server Logs

Like standard web platforms, our servers automatically record anonymous technical log entries when you visit our site. This data may include browser user-agent strings, referring URLs, device operating systems, and anonymous timestamps. We also employ Google Analytics 4 (GA4) solely to measure aggregated geographic engagement trends and performance metrics. All analytics data is anonymized and never linked to personal profiles.

4. Local Storage (Client-Side Storage)

To ensure lightning-fast 0ms performance and offline convenience, VORA AI saves your chosen travel preferences (e.g., selected destination, language setting, dark/light theme, and bookmarked travel spots) in your browser's localStorage. This data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to private remote tracking databases.

5. External Links to Verified Partners

Our platform may display informative outbound links or booking options to official services such as Korea Tourism Organization, Klook, or Agoda. We encourage visitors to review the independent privacy practices of any external partner platforms they choose to visit.
